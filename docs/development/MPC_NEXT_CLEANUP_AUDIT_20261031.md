Evidence: `live_runs/mpc_collision_margin_experiment_20261030/cpp_cleanup2_qp_parity_20261031.json`, `cpp_cleanup2_fullreplay_parity_20261031.json`.

However, the live practice run `practice_cpp_cleanup2_20261031_01` **did not pass**: it ended after **five scored laps** on a genuine source-model corridor rejection at stage 2, with eight recoveries and a maximum callback of **19.217 ms**, and no publication deadline misses. The shutdown was fail-closed with no unsafe command. Since this experiment was not a demonstrated robust improvement, both edits were **reverted together** to the previous fully validated version. The failure is *not* proof that the edits caused it; stochastic state/timing effects are possible. Do not claim this experiment successful or silently ship it. Repeat controlled A/B and stress/failure-state replay before reintroducing either optimization.
