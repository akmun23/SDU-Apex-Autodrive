# SQP model agreement and failure-state audit

Date: 2026-11-02. The changes in this audit add measurement, source-derived curvature and optional agreement-driven globalization. They are not a qualified production solver replacement.

## Changes

- Each R1 and R2 pass now records its own QP-predicted cost reduction, exact source-plant cost reduction when the full rollout is available, per-stage maximum state error, per-stage footprint discrepancy, source footprint violation, line-search trials and selected alpha. R2 is measured against its own physical relinearization.
- The controller rejection record now contains both delayed steering and throttle queues, all 22 hidden source-plant values, initialization flags, incoming branch state and wheel-slip inputs.
- `minimum_sqp_model_agreement_ratio` optionally rejects a full step when its exact source improvement explains too little of the QP-predicted improvement. A positive threshold also enables a persistent scalar step radius in `[0.0625, 1]`: failed or weakly agreeing passes shrink it; an accepted damped step caps it at the accepted alpha; strong agreement grows it. A rejected step still needs an exact-valid damped candidate before it can be used. R2 relinearizes from the accepted source trajectory when the cycle budget allows.
- `capture_sqp_prediction_metrics` is an offline diagnostic mode. It continues the current radius-limited QP proposal through the source plant with diagnostic-only relaxed map bounds, then scores it with the original frozen objective and corridor. It does not affect acceptance. This extra source rollout measured about 3–9 ms in replay, so production profiles leave it off.
- `source_informed_sqp` uses a source-gradient directional secant to add positive input curvature at R2. This is source-derived curvature information, but it is not a second-order dynamics Hessian. The solver already uses stagewise affine state/input dynamics; full second-order multiple-shooting SQP remains open. Curvature and agreement globalization remain off in canonical profiles because complete-race evidence is insufficient.

## Evidence

### Saved QP directions

On the saved `corner303fail` state, the un-gated full R1 proposal predicted a 37,745.6 cost decrease but achieved 7,195.2 in the exact source model (agreement 0.191). An agreement floor of 0.2 selected alpha 0.5 and reduced the exact source objective from 9,297.7 to 6,153.5. With source-gradient secant curvature plus the gate, that state reached 2,962.1 offline. On the saved practice50 state, a 0.25 floor rejected a useful step; 0.2 was the least blunt floor tested. These are saved-state results, not race qualification. The machine-readable comparisons are in `live_runs/mpc_sqp_agreement_gate_audit_20261102_corrected/summary.csv` and `live_runs/mpc_sqp_curvature_agreement_audit_20261102/summary.csv`.

### Practice failure state

The previously successful near-wall profile reached 11 laps in its earlier run (`live_runs/forensic_nearwall25_practice_20261102_05`). Its strict rerun reached three laps, then rejected the horizon at stage 11. The live R1 predicted a 26,565.1 cost reduction but crossed the hard corridor; the complete source-exact reduction is unavailable because the live safety rollout stops at that first violation. Its partial stage diagnostics show maximum state error 9.88 at stage 11, state coordinate 4; maximum footprint discrepancy 0.404 m at stage 12; and source footprint violation 0.010 m at stage 12. Only one line-search trial ran before alpha-zero fallback, and R2 did not run. The strict run report is `live_runs/mpc_sqp_preserve_practice_20261102_01/mpc_run_report.json`.

An offline replay was initialized with the failure's complete actuator queue and hidden state, with the saved nominal controls and a cold solver state. With raw-proposal capture on, this *replayed* R1 predicted a 35,148.2 decrease while the exact source objective increased by 85,068.7 (agreement -2.42); maximum state error was 47.32, footprint discrepancy 0.969 m, and source footprint violation 0.480 m. This replay does not reconstruct the live ADMM warm state and therefore is mechanism evidence, not the exact failed live QP step. Its raw metrics are in `live_runs/mpc_sqp_preserve_practice_20261102_01/failure_replay/capture_stderr.txt`.

The current physical state was not shown to be unrecoverable. Ten of 29 fixed 750 ms steering/throttle continuations passed exact source validation from that same state. Low-throttle left-steering plans were feasible; neutral and right-steering plans failed. This points to a bad QP direction at a recoverable state. The tested plans and results are in `live_runs/mpc_sqp_preserve_practice_20261102_01/failure_replay/recovery_sweep_*`.

For the exact Unity state at that practice failure epoch, one-step source-versus-Unity error was small: forward-speed error 0.0231 m/s with transition-only residual -0.00020 m/s, lateral-speed error 0.00344 m/s and yaw-rate error 0.02499 rad/s. Hidden wheel-state differences were below 0.001 rad/s. Nearby full 750 ms windows also matched well (663 valid windows; 28 exceeded 5 cm). The bag stopped about 0.22 s after rejection, so there is no full Unity horizon from the failure epoch. See `failure_replay/source_one_step.csv`, `source_rollout_cases.json` and `source_plant_replay.txt`.

### Agreement-driven practice trial

With the prior near-wall overlay plus `source_informed_sqp: true` and agreement floor `0.2`, the strict 11-total-lap practice trial reached seven laps, then stopped on one rejected nonlinear horizon. It did not reproduce the earlier 11-lap successful run. Of 1,525 measured R1 passes, 1,470 had complete exact-source proposals; their raw agreement median was 0.22. Selected-step agreement median was 0.66 for R1 and 0.92 for 879 measured R2 passes. R1 used backtracking in 748 passes and selected alpha zero 77 times. The controller converged in 1,393 solver passes, hit its iteration cap in 127 and hit the ADMM time budget in six. These counts show better screened/selected steps, but also substantial backtracking and no repeatable complete practice run. Practice feedback rollout was enabled, so these live agreement ratios compare the QP prediction to the source trajectory including feedback; they are not a pure dynamics-only ratio.

At the final practice rejection (progress 214.687 m), R1 predicted a 1,028.5 cost decrease, then the exact source rollout crossed the corridor at stage 8. Over the checked prefix, maximum state error was 5.6 mm; maximum footprint discrepancy was 0.168 m and source footprint violation was 12.9 mm. R1 converged (primal residual 3.8e-6, dual 8.0e-5), but exact line search was skipped for lack of cycle budget, and R2 did not run. This was a near-boundary footprint failure with no remaining time to validate a damped rescue.

At that exact Unity state, one-step source error was larger than in the earlier practice case: speed error 0.0403 m/s with transition-only residual 0.0161 m/s, lateral-speed error 0.0110 m/s and yaw-rate error 0.156 rad/s. Two rear wheel-rate errors were 4.49 and 5.25 rad/s. Eight of 28 fixed source continuations passed from the recorded state, so feasible source continuations remained. This supports a corridor/step-selection failure at a recoverable source state, with rear wheel-state prediction mismatch as a contributing uncertainty. The exact 750 ms Unity continuation after the fault is unavailable.

A separate cold-state offline replay used the saved nominal controls and complete hidden state but did not reconstruct the live ADMM warm state. It predicted a 5,946 cost decrease and achieved 5,685 in a complete source rollout (agreement 0.956). That replay stayed inside the source corridor and is not the live rejected step. The large difference from the live 1,028-cost proposal demonstrates that the missing warm solver state matters. Artifacts are in `live_runs/mpc_sqp_agreement_practice_20261102_01/failure_replay/`.

A second strict practice trial lowered the agreement floor to `0.15`. It stopped before its first lap on a solver deadline before ADMM at progress 27.606 m. Among 143 recorded cycles, 80 solver passes converged, 21 hit the iteration cap, eight hit the ADMM time budget and two reached the deadline before ADMM. This did not establish a viable lower-threshold setting. The report and full cycle sidecar are in `live_runs/mpc_sqp_agreement_practice_015_20261102_01/`.

### Competition failure state

The strict IROS trial collided after 3.82 s without completing a lap. Its final rejected R1 predicted a 75,986.1 cost decrease, failed the source corridor at the first transition, and left a 0.145 m footprint violation at stage 1. Maximum state error was 8.83 at stage 1; four line-search trials failed and R2 did not run. At the recorded state, lateral error was -0.538 m, heading error -0.244 rad, yaw rate 1.612 rad/s, steering was saturated at +0.524 rad, and the delayed actuator commands and all hidden plant state are recorded in the diagnostics. The race report is `live_runs/mpc_sqp_canonical_competition_20261102_01/mpc_run_report.json`.

One-step source-versus-Unity prediction near the final state was close, but the full competition bag gives mixed attribution: 123 complete 750 ms windows, 50 windows with invalid Unity pose/twist closure, and 21 source-motion-dominated endpoint discrepancies among the classifiable windows. A complete source-versus-Unity horizon at the collision state is unavailable because the simulator stopped. The collision state was already near or outside the planned corridor with saturated steering and high wheel slip; no continuation sweep was run there. Do not attribute this collision solely to SQP or claim the state was recoverable. Details are in `source_one_step.csv`, `source_rollout_cases.json` and `source_plant_replay.txt`.

### Agreement-driven competition trial

The strict 11-total-lap IROS run with the same `0.2` agreement floor and source-gradient curvature stopped before its first lap at progress 58.832 m. R1 predicted a 658.1 cost decrease, but the source check marked the command-start state outside the corridor by 26.1 mm at stage 0; four line-search trials also failed at stage 0. R1 had reached the minimum step radius, and ADMM stopped at its 150-iteration cap with residuals 0.277/0.371. R2 did not run. The measured state already lay outside the strict source corridor, so shrinking the QP correction could not restore feasibility at the command-start epoch. All 28 fixed steering/throttle plans tested from the recorded state failed source validation at stage 0. This is evidence of an already nonrecoverable state under that corridor check, but it does not prove a Unity collision or exclude source-model error.

The last available source-versus-Unity 25 ms transition before rejection had small body-state residuals: speed error 0.0032 m/s, transition-only speed residual 0.0048 m/s, lateral-speed error 0.00017 m/s and yaw-rate error 0.0015 rad/s. Rear wheel-rate errors were 6.81 and 6.55 rad/s. There is no Unity sample at the predicted command-start epoch, so exact source-versus-Unity agreement at stage 0 is unverified. A cold offline replay of the saved state and nominal (without live ADMM warm memory) predicted a 5,746 cost decrease and achieved 3,591, but still violated the later source corridor by 92.9 mm. See `live_runs/mpc_sqp_agreement_competition_20261102_01/failure_replay/` and `source_one_step.csv`.

## Validation and disposition

- `colcon build --packages-select f1tenth_mpc`: passed.
- `cmake --build build/f1tenth_mpc --target mpc_bag_solver_replay -j4`: passed.
- `colcon test --packages-select f1tenth_mpc` and `colcon test-result --verbose`: passed, 308 tests, 0 failures.
- Strict practice at agreement floor 0.2: failed after seven total laps (six scored). At floor 0.15: solver deadline failure before the first lap. The canonical practice profile failed in a separate rerun after six laps, and the earlier successful near-wall result remains unreproduced.
- Strict competition with agreement globalization and source-gradient curvature: stopped before the first lap on a source-corridor rejection. The earlier canonical competition run collided before its first lap. No complete competition race was validated.

No canonical profile was changed. The agreement floor, adaptive step radius and raw capture remain opt-in. These results do not qualify a new globalization setting, source-curvature setting, practice profile or competition race. Selected-step agreement improved in the practice run, but deadline-limited backtracking and a stage-0 competition infeasibility remain. Full second-order multiple-shooting SQP and repeatable complete practice/competition races remain open work.
