## IROS physical collision root cause (not AMCL)
- Recorded raw Unity position and IPS jumped at exactly the same source update as collision_count 0→1 in iros_7075_reuseqpcore_20261031_01 after 5 lap crossings. Full mapped body clearance before collision decreased ~0.118m → 0.072m → 0.043m, violating 0.05m mandatory margin. Frenet corridor alone did not encode that real wall accurately. Enabling independent full map authority reveals future violations but, without compatible optimizer constraints, the MPC can still arrive at a state with no verified 750ms continuation.
- A normal map-aware SQP must explicitly optimize ahead of real physical map boundaries and consider viable terminal states, not merely reject geometric collisions after the SQP has solved a different constraint set.
